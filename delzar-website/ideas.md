**Idea A — Sovereign Authority (Dark Editorial)**

- **Design Movement:** Dark editorial meets federal gravitas — think Bloomberg Government meets Indigenous sovereignty
- **Core Principles:** Authority through darkness, gold as power, typography-first hierarchy, data as identity
- **Color Philosophy:** Deep navy (#0A1628) base with gold (#C9A84C) as the only accent — every gold element signals importance. White text on dark creates institutional authority. The darkness conveys that this is serious federal business, not a startup.

- **Interaction Philosophy:** Deliberate, weighty — hover states reveal gold underlays, not color changes. Scroll-triggered counter animations for stats.
- **Animation:** Slow, authoritative entrances (600ms ease-out). Gold shimmer on key CTAs. No bouncy or playful motion.

## CHOSEN DESIGN: Idea A — Sovereign Authority (Dark Editorial)

**Rationale:** Delzar competes for federal IT contracts against large primes. The dark editorial aesthetic signals institutional authority and federal-grade professionalism. The gold accent directly references tribal heritage and sovereignty without being decorative. The Playfair Display + IBM Plex Sans pairing creates a unique voice — editorial gravitas meets federal precision — that no competitor in the tribal GovCon space currently uses.
