# Coffeyville-ring govcon gap strategy

USAspending answers who already got paid.
SAM registration answers who is allowed to bid.
City/KDOT license lists answer who can actually do the work.

Join those three. A SAM logo is not revenue.

## Four buckets

| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| WINNER | Prime or notable sub dollars on USAspending / FPDS / KDOT | Teammate, sub to them, or capture their next recompete |
| SAM_IDLE | Has a UEI, little or no recent prime obligations | They can bid today and are not |
| DARK | Licensed locally, no usable SAM record | Leaving federal money on the table |
| BUYER | City, USD, hospital, college on SAM to receive grants | Customer, not competitor |

## Query order

1. `scripts/usaspending_coffeyville.py`
2. `scripts/classify_govcon_gap.py`
3. Optional SAM Entity API v4 when `SAM_API_KEY` is set
